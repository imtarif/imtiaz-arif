Academic profile

Leadership grounded in scholarship.

Prof. Imtiaz Arif is a Professor and Dean of the Faculty of Management Sciences at DHA Suffa University, Karachi. His career in higher education includes academic, administrative and strategic leadership, faculty development, programme and curriculum oversight, institutional planning, accreditation, and research management.

Before joining DHA Suffa University, he served at Iqra University from January 2000 to December 2024 in roles including Campus Director, Associate Dean and academic staff member. His work has included establishing and operationalising the Gulshan Campus, contributing to university ranking initiatives, and leading academic and administrative operations.

As a researcher, his work examines financial markets, financial development, banking, economic policy, and the ways technology and innovation influence organisations, consumers and society.
